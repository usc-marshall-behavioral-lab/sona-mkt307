What to Do

Six steps, start to finish. Tap a box to check it off, and this page remembers.

Your progress0 of 6 complete

By Sept 18

Create your SONA account

Go to marshall-mkt.sona-systems.com and click Request Account. Use your USC NetID, not your full email and not your student ID. A confirmation email with a temporary password follows, so reset it right away, then note the 5-digit SONA ID on your profile. The lab uses it to look up your credits.

By Sept 18

Complete the prescreening questionnaire

SONA prompts you for it at first login. It asks about demographics, interests and experiences so you get matched to studies you are eligible for. There are no right or wrong answers.

Those two steps together are what registers you. An account on its own is not enough, and no study invitations arrive until prescreening is finished.

After prescreening

Watch for study invitations

Invitations go out by email to the address on your SONA profile, so make sure it is one you read. Spots are first come, first served across all sections, so sign up as soon as one arrives.

Through the semester

Complete 6 online studies

About 10 minutes each, 0.5 credits each, on a laptop or desktop rather than a phone. Signing up is not a reservation: start and finish the study in one sitting, or the sign-up is canceled and no credit posts. Run through the online study checklist before you start.

Oct 12 to Nov 13

Attend one lab session

One 60-minute session at VPD LL102, worth 1.0 credit. Book it in SONA, arrive 10 minutes early, and cancel at least 8 hours ahead if plans change. A second session earns nothing, since the same studies are used. The in-person session checklist covers the day itself.

By Dec 4

Reach 4.0 credits

Credits post within 24 hours of each study unless the study page says otherwise, and your running total is on your SONA profile. Check it as you go rather than at the end.

4.0CREDITS

That is the whole semester goal, due Friday, December 4. Extra online studies do not replace the lab session.

Why the sign-up rules differ

A lab seat is a physical place in a room at a fixed time, so booking it holds it. An online study is a link in a queue, and holding one open would keep another student out of a slot nobody is using. That is the single most common reason a credit does not post: a student signs up for an online study meaning to come back to it later, and the sign-up is removed before they do.

Extra online studies cannot replace the lab session. Both parts are required on their own, no matter how many online studies you complete.

How invitations workWhy they arrive when they do

How you hear about a study

When a study is posted, an invitation goes out by email to every registered student in BUAD 307, roughly a thousand people. Spots are first come, first served, so the gap between the invitation landing and the study filling can be short. Enough total spots are scheduled across the term for everyone who registers by the deadline to finish the requirement.

Notifications come by email only

SONA sends to the address on your SONA profile, not necessarily the one you check most. If invitations are not reaching you, check that address first, then your spam folder, and add the SONA sender to your contacts.

Why you might not qualify for a particular study

Your prescreening answers are used to match you with studies you are eligible for, so some studies will not appear for you, and SONA also keeps you out of a study that conflicts with one you have already taken. Neither affects your ability to reach 4.0. There are 11 online studies and you need 6.

Rules and cancelingHow to take part, and what to do if plans change

While taking a study

  • Answer honestly and attentively. Studies may include attention checks.
  • Close other tabs and silence your phone for online studies.
  • Arrive 10 minutes early for a lab session. Doors close at the start time and late arrivals cannot be admitted.
  • Seating in the lab is assigned at check-in.
  • If something breaks mid-study, email the lab straight away rather than restarting.

Canceling a lab session

Excused

  • Canceled in SONA at least 8 hours before the start time
  • The seat is released to another student
  • You can book a later session

Unexcused

  • Canceled with less than 8 hours notice, or not at all
  • Counts as a no-show
  • The seat goes unused, which is why the window exists

Walk-ins. If a seat opens from a no-show, walk-ins may be admitted. Wait outside about five minutes after check-in, and be ready to confirm you have not already attended this term.

Partial completion

You earn credit for each study you complete, and partial completion earns partial credit. Nothing you finish is lost if you fall short of 4.0. What changes is how much of the research participation portion of your grade you receive.

How much that portion is worth, and exactly how your credits convert into it, is set by your instructor in the course syllabus rather than by the lab. Check your syllabus first, and ask your instructor about anything to do with the grade itself.

There are no extensions. Credits not earned by December 4 cannot be made up afterward, and there is no grace period.

Why this matters for 307How do marketers know what consumers will do?

Marketing decisions rest on evidence about how people actually respond, and that evidence comes from studies like these. The path runs in four steps, and you are the third one.

1

Question

A Marshall researcher wants to know how consumers respond to something.

2

Study

They design a study to test it, and the USC IRB reviews and approves it.

3

You

Your responses are the data. Thoughtful, attentive answers are what make the result mean anything.

4

Evidence

The findings feed research that reaches publication and, in time, the classroom.

Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw from any study at any time without affecting your grade or standing. Responses are confidential, are never shared with your instructor, and are not linked to your identity in the data. Every study is approved by the USC Institutional Review Board.

FAQ

The questions the lab answers most often.

Account and login problemsWrong site, cannot log in, no confirmation email

I cannot log in.

Check that you are using your USC NetID rather than your student ID number, and that you are on the yellow MKT site rather than the blue one. Use the "Lost your password?" link to reset. If it still fails, email the lab.

How do I know I am on the right SONA site?

The BUAD 307 site has a yellow accent bar and reads "Marketing (MKT) – Research Participation for BUAD 307" in the header. USC runs more than one SONA system, and credits do not transfer between them.

No confirmation email arrived.

Check your spam folder first, then confirm you used your NetID rather than your full email address, then try the account request again. If nothing works, email the lab with the NetID you used.

Can I use credits from BUAD 304?

No. BUAD 304 runs a separate program on a different SONA site, and credits do not carry across courses.

Invitations and sessionsNothing arriving, studies filling up, missed a session

I am not getting invitations.

First confirm prescreening is actually finished, since nothing is sent until it is. Then check the email address on your SONA profile, and your spam folder.

Studies fill up before I can sign up.

Sign up as soon as the invitation arrives rather than later the same day. Enough total spots are scheduled for everyone registered by the deadline. For lab sessions, you can also try arriving in person to claim a no-show seat.

Why was my online sign-up canceled?

Online sign-ups are not reservations. If you sign up and do not finish the study right away, the sign-up is removed and no credit posts. Start and complete it in one sitting.

I missed my lab session.

Book another one from the remaining session weeks as soon as you can, since there are only 4 of them. Cancelling at least 8 hours ahead next time keeps it excused. If SONA will not let you sign up again, email the lab.

Credits and gradesMissing credits, partial credit, the alternative assignment

My credits are not showing up.

Credits usually post within 24 hours unless the study page says otherwise. If it has been longer, email the lab with your SONA ID and the study name and date.

What if I do not finish everything?

You keep every credit you earn, and partial completion earns partial credit. How those credits translate into your course grade is set by your instructor in the syllabus, so check there first.

Can extra online studies replace the lab session?

No. The lab session is required on its own and cannot be substituted.

I am under 18, or I missed the registration deadline.

A written paper assignment is available instead. Contact your course instructor for the topic, length, due date and how it is weighted.

I have accommodations that affect participation.

Contact Prof. Kristin Diehl at kdiehl@marshall.usc.edu.
